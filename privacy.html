Privacy Policy

Concept Quizzer

Last updated: October 22, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Concept Quizzer. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit or use our educational website.

We aim to handle personal information responsibly and use it only for appropriate purposes related to the website and its features.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, the website may process the following categories of information:

  • Account information provided through Google sign-in, such as your name, email address, and account identifier, where made available by the authentication service.
  • Learning progress and settings, such as quiz progress or preferences, if these features are enabled.
  • Technical information processed by the website hosting provider or other services used to operate the website.

The exact information processed depends on the features you use and the configuration of the services connected to Concept Quizzer.

3. Google Sign-In

Concept Quizzer may offer Google sign-in so users can authenticate their accounts. Google processes information according to its own privacy policy and the permissions requested during sign-in.

We do not ask users to share their Google passwords directly with Concept Quizzer.

For more information, please read Google's Privacy Policy .

4. Learning Progress and Cloud Backup

If cloud progress backup is enabled, certain account-related learning data may be stored using Firebase services. This can help users access their progress across sessions or devices, depending on the website's implementation.

Information stored in the cloud is subject to the configuration of the relevant Firebase services and the applicable service terms.

5. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide account sign-in and authentication.
  • Save or restore learning progress when supported.
  • Maintain and improve website functionality.
  • Respond to feedback and technical problems.
  • Protect the website against misuse and security issues.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

The website may use browser local storage to remember settings or learning progress. Authentication providers and other integrated services may also use technologies such as cookies where necessary for their operation.

You can manage certain browser storage and cookie settings through your browser. Changing these settings may affect some website features.

7. Third-Party Services

Concept Quizzer may rely on third-party services, including GitHub Pages for hosting, Google for sign-in, and Firebase for authentication or data storage.

These providers may process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

  • GitHub Privacy Policies
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security

8. Data Security

We aim to use appropriate settings and practices to protect information handled by the website. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Concept Quizzer is an educational website that may be used by school students. Students should use the website responsibly and should not share passwords, home addresses, phone numbers, or other sensitive personal information in public areas.

Parents or guardians can help children understand online privacy and contact us with relevant concerns.

10. Your Choices

Depending on the features available, you may be able to stop using an account, manage browser storage, or contact us with questions about personal information. Some information may remain with service providers according to their policies and applicable requirements.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when website features or data practices change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, please use the contact details provided on our Contact page.
